## New direction: preserve local evidence, then test corroboration

**Hypothesis:** small lesions can be present in the encoded image while global
slice compression/averaging loses evidence useful to a target. Current attention
already differs by target; merely adding 'attention' is not a new experiment.

A new optional signed **patch-witness residual** is now implemented in the
canonical model/trainer/inference. It scores DINO patch tokens by target before
pooling, averages the strongest4 patch scores per slice and strongest2 genuine
slices per series, then retains the strongest genuine series, alongside the
existing global-context classifier. It consumes the same encoded MRI views;
there is no second encoder/teacher, extra crop pass or patch-level disease label.
Local output starts at zero; an independent branch RNG preserves shared head
initialization. Zero-weight control and one-weight candidate extract identical
patch features and have identical initialized learned tensors. All policy/source
bindings are checkpointed; padded/NaN observations cannot become witnesses.

The implementation has local numerical, padding, gradient, BF16 and real-trainer
synthetic resume checks. **It has not run on real MRI/H100 or demonstrated any
accuracy improvement.** Top-k may amplify artifacts, unstable early selection,
normal anatomy or duplicated views. A diffuse disease may need extent rather
than a single witness. These are falsifiable failure modes, not details to hide.

Primary next experiment: bounded actual official-weight/MRI engineering recovery,
then a separately funded fixed-budget paired fit with identical original
membership, initialization, observations and outer masks. Report all12 targets,
artifact/missing-series stress behavior and paired component uncertainty at the
predeclared endpoint. Reproduce a promising gain on another seed/fold before
promotion. Keep the31-cell overlay as a **separate** contrast; combining it with
witness pooling would prevent attribution.

Next mechanisms worth research, not yet implemented:

1. **Corroborating witnesses:** shared DINO features that compare target-specific
   evidence across slices/acquisitions; require supporting context while allowing
   one-plane lesions. Shuffled-acquisition and equal-budget random-evidence
   controls distinguish anatomy from extra parameters. Do not pretend unregistered
   planes are spatially aligned.
2. **Finding presence versus clinical threshold:** separate an auxiliary head for
   descriptive morphology from the competition's severity/acuity target. Mild
   fluid or chronic ligament change should not be relabeled as a positive target.
   Report supervision must be independently admitted on fitting-only cases.
3. **Missingness-aware learning:** test whether report selection and reliability
   holds induce biased negatives/observations. Quantify admissible assumptions
   before positive-unlabeled risk or confident pseudo-labeling. Component
   cross-fitting is needed for any teacher; agreement is not truth.
4. **Adaptive observation:** spend a bounded encoder budget on regions requested
   by task queries while retaining full context and exploration. Compare uniform,
   random and learned selection with equal total encoder pixels/tokens. Current
   full-frame resolution alone did not establish the benefit.
5. **Physical relationships:** the existing slice-distance auxiliary prototype
   has an actual training-only gradient-scale diagnostic, but no accepted real
   production-trainer GPU recovery/full clinical comparison. Do not call it a
   trained anatomical model.

## Literature anchors and the questions for deep research

- [Attention2Minority](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37890421/) studies small
  lesions diluted in whole-slide MIL bags and selects salient instances. This
  motivates a failure hypothesis across domains; it does not prove the mechanism
  applies to knee MRI or validate our specific top-k rule.
- [MAD-MIL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v254/keshvarikhojasteh24a.html) investigates
  multiple attention heads for weakly supervised slide classification. We already
  have target-specific heads; duplicating that feature is not sufficient novelty.
- [KneeDINO](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/EMERGE_021.html) uses task-specific
  cross-plane fusion and three RadioDINO encoders; its MRNet three-task AUCs are
  not twelve-target RSNA competition scores. Our desired mechanism uses one
  shared DINO encoder.
- [CoPAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11368947/) provides an anatomy/
  multi-plane context direction on another cohort; do not compare its reported
  AUC numerically to ours.
- [Official Jev description](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev)
  concerns structured decisions. Its available announcement is not a reproducible
  MRI backbone result. Jev is not JEPA; output validity is not diagnostic accuracy.

Please investigate **mechanisms and implementable experiments**, not an ensemble
shopping list or another recommendation to increase rank/epochs. Specifically:

- Is our weak-label selection/severity mismatch large enough that a correct
  visual encoder is optimizing the wrong statistical problem? What clean
  fitting-only evidence would distinguish that from a representation bottleneck?
- How can one shared pretrained2D DINO learn lesion evidence, three-dimensional
  context and contrast-specific contradictions under examination-level labels,
  without falsely assigning a diagnosis to every slice or inventing alignment?
- Which sparse/extent/corroboration operator preserves focal evidence yet rejects
  acquisition artifacts and common anatomy? What controlled counterexample would
  disprove each candidate before a full training run?
- Can report morphology support a hierarchical presence→severity/acuity task
  graph while leaving inference MRI-only and all protected labels unused?
- What is a credible independent validation design when MCL has only two positive
  development components and masks are severity-dependent? Do not hide undefined
  bootstrap draws or use leaderboard tuning as confirmation.
- Which ideas would plausibly create a large gain, what exact data/compute do they
  require, and what would make us stop investing after a negative experiment?
