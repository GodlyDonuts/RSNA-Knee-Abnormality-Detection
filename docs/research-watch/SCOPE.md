# Publication scope and completeness

## Included

All 33 entries of the delivered dossier are accounted for: 32 text files are preserved, with three relative-link targets repaired across two files; the Word memo is represented by its original companion Markdown and a complete document-order paragraph/table-cell text extraction. Additional relevant research reports, ledger drafts, metadata inventories, classification/provenance registers, synthetic scripts and synthetic results in the research workspace are included. The late-findings addendum preserves the subsequent factual research screen.

## Deduplication and transformations

Byte-identical dossier/workspace copies map to a single file, with all source aliases listed in MANIFEST.json. Original research prose is retained. The dossier README's Word link points to its Markdown equivalent. The observation-strategy report's experiment-ledger and synthetic-counterexample links point to their relocated copies. Word layout is omitted from the plain-text extraction; substantive paragraphs and table-cell text are retained. No model inference or scientific recalculation was performed during packaging.

## Intentionally excluded

- Raw clinical records, MRI/DICOM pixels, patient identifiers, protected validation rows, per-case labels or predictions, weights, credentials and authentication material
- Unrelated application/job-search files and unrelated project artifacts
- Internal assistant memory, operational instructions and raw internal worker messages
- Duplicate copies of the competition repository's source and tests; pinned references and source hashes remain available
- Downloaded third-party full papers or copied external implementations; source links and research assessments are preserved instead
- Word/PDF rendering images, layout-only document-builder code and duplicate binary packaging; complete research text is provided

The audit inventory contains directory/blob metadata and source links, not case records. Aggregate metrics and synthetic arrays remain included. Preserved historical paths are contextual provenance, not a claim that those environments are available.

## Boundaries of completeness

This archive is complete for the available research artifacts listed in the manifest and the supplied late factual findings. It does not claim recovery of deleted files, every historical proposal revision as a separate original file, inaccessible private receipts, unobserved runs, or a complete reading of every repository audit directory. Missing evidence remains marked as missing rather than reconstructed as a result.
