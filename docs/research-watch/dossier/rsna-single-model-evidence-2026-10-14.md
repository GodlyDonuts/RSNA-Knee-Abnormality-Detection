# RSNA Knee: single-model evidence and recipe parity

Verified 2026-10-14, approximately 18:30–18:40 UTC; label-lineage correction added18:48UTC. Read-only research using primary author discussions, runnable notebook source, displayed Kaggle score/version receipts, dataset terms, official rules and NDA documentation. No model/data downloads, training, submissions or author contact were performed. See the separate `rsna-steven-v6-provenance-addendum-2026-10-14.md` for the verified current-target ancestry.

## Bottom line

The premise is partly supported. Several current competitors explicitly report **single-fold models above 0.950**, including small ConvNeXt-T and DINOv2-S. Their strongest disclosed common ingredient is image-derived pseudo-supervision, not an unusually large encoder. However, none of those >0.950 claims audited here exposes the exact trained checkpoint, complete training recipe and independently matched score receipt. TTA, epoch averaging and teacher composition remain undisclosed for most.

The strongest directly inspectable **one learned inference-state** baseline is nartaa's **0.949**, now backed by a public runnable notebook's Kaggle score badge, pinned version, checkpoint hash, inference source and explicit adaptation permission. It is one SWA state averaged from three epochs, with two anatomical inference views. It is not a single raw epoch or single pass. Original Raptor **0.924** is the clearer strict single-epoch, no-TTA public example. A public **0.950 Apex** notebook and **0.956 Helios** notebook are actual ensembles.

Do not infer that all high scores are disguised ensembles, or that an architecture change alone will close the gap. Conversely, do not equate the current public soft-label overlay with verified fold-safe image pseudo-labeling: subsequent exact-byte receipts and live documentation identify it as Steven v4's blend of two report-labeller probability sources, not a documented MRI teacher.

## 1. Definitions and evidence levels

- **Single raw checkpoint:** one trained epoch/state used at inference, without parameter averaging
- **Single learned inference state:** one set of learned parameters loaded; may be SWA/EMA/soup derived from multiple epochs
- **Single architecture:** may still mean five independently trained fold checkpoints; not single-state inference
- **Single-state with TTA:** one parameter state, multiple image views and prediction aggregation
- **Distilled single:** one inference state, trained using one or more image teachers; training complexity and supervision ancestry must still be disclosed
- **A:** platform score badge/version plus inspected executable source and named assets; this research did not rerun it
- **B:** primary author's explicit score/definition statement, without a matched public executable/checkpoint receipt
- **C:** repository or notebook-title claim without platform score corroboration

**Source-code caution:** nartaa's `_eval_centers` enumerates centers over the whole concatenated volume and uses neighboring indices. It is not a guarantee that every triplet consists of physically neighboring slices in the same acquired series: boundary-crossing triplets occur. Its mirror function uses the central slice's slot to choose channel reversal for sagittal views or width reversal for coronal/axial views. Preserve exact semantics when reproducing; distinguish deliberate reproduction from a corrected alternative.

The native and nartaa pipelines both nominally select96 source images. Therefore “the smaller model sees far more slices” is unsupported. The defensible hypotheses are different **anatomic allocation, through-stack span, physical field of view, neighbor-channel context, per-visit training coverage, dense test aggregation, supervision and adaptation**. A matched experiment is needed to attribute gain.

- Pinned scored environment: `gcr.io/kaggle-private-byod/python@sha256:2757e0c7d1e0a9cb43da657b97e223c321a98f5014bdf64f44f2f6b083ad2b2f`; qualification reports torch2.11.0+cu128 and timm1.0.29, two T4s available. Exact live policy controls CUDA workers

### Original Raptor training is useful but not a complete score reproduction

[Training notebook V8 /347608187](https://www.kaggle.com/code/dreaddevelopment/knee-mri-training-the-twelve-finding-model?scriptVersionId=347608187) exposes AdamW, separate backbone/head rates3e−5/1e−3, weight decay.02, OneCycle with15% warmup, gradient clip3, BCE positive weighting clipped1–10, 12train/24evaluation windows, full backbone fine-tuning, and a target-specific MIL attention head without the native128-dimensional bottleneck. The example discusses16epochs and ~3hours on an RTX4090; batch8/gradient checkpointing uses about23.7GB, smaller batches are suggested for16GB cards.

Pitfalls: trainer default normalization is `none`, whereas scored inference uses ImageNet normalization; default must be overridden to reproduce that contract. Training autocast is bfloat16-oriented, not a drop-in T4 FP16/scaler setup. The public44-slice training cache and64-slice score input differ. Best epoch and top-three checkpoints are selected using reused Gold58. A static11-second notebook execution is not a training-run proof. Code must be read through actual constants, not historical docstrings claiming a self-supervised encoder or other obsolete architecture.

## 7. Actionable gaps and next decision gates

1. **Use the now-verified target ancestry.** Subsequent exact-byte receipts identify the current Shio-Koube/knee3 overlay as Steven datasetv6's v4 blend: authorv2 report labels +lixin73 GPT-5.6-Sol report labels, probability-space blend, upstreamCC0. The inspected Data Card documents no MRI teacher. Exact coefficients/extraction settings remain incomplete, and Gold58 guided the report proxy/blend recipe. An OOF image-teacher experiment would introduce a different signal; this existing overlay does not establish it. See the provenance addendum
2. **Separate coverage from target correction.** The earlier2247-row fitting audit changed13760 observed cells to26964 and changed13750 old observed values; nearly all public targets were fractional. These are that audit's counts, not a fresh all3403-row measurement. This combined missing-cell filling, target replacement and softening. A fixed original-mask comparison and separately admitted missing-cell pseudo-targets answer different questions
   - Follow-up verification: this full-public overlay gives every admitted cell weight1, while the now-identified Steven source defines0.5 as not-addressed and recommends `2*abs(p−0.5)` confidence. Current BCE therefore pulls uncertain targets toward0.5 instead of dropping them. Compare both semantics under controlled conditions; do not call source-recommended weighting a proven improvement or confuse it with positive-class weighting
3. **Use a controlled image-teacher experiment, not blind overlay replacement.** Keep patient/group leakage exclusions; generate OOF teacher probabilities on fitting rows; retain immutable native labels and masks; compare fractional blends against report-only and current-overlay controls. Prefer target-specific uncertainty review and preregistered modest weights. Do not copy competitors' Gold58 tuning because our protected-gold boundary is mandatory
4. **Establish the executable one-state baseline before adapting it, if approved.** Preserve source/checkpoint hashes, license snapshot and target order; first reproduce original preprocessing and predictions, then change one factor. A fine-tuned checkpoint is a new score claim; .949 is not inherited automatically. Crucially, all4349-row upstream training likely includes our internal protected weak-development rows, and Gold58 guided upstream selection. Compare its exposure manifest against our protected groups before admission: excluding those rows only during our subsequent fine-tune does not erase upstream exposure or create independent validation
5. **Treat training population as a confound.** Our3403 fitting rows versus competitors4349 non-gold rows is a946-row gap. Audit whether any additional rows can be admitted without violating protected group/holdout boundaries. Never simply train on all4349 to copy a competitor
6. **Run matched adaptation and representation controls.** Full trunk fine-tuning vs rank32; triplet vs repeated grayscale; physical crop vs full field; sparse one-group source-slot training vs bag training; identical targets/split/budget. The .878 source-slot result does not reject CoAtNet, 2.5D or source-faithful training generally
7. **Do not spend further quota on epoch averaging without new evidence.** Prior epochs6–10 averaging did not improve this native run. That does not invalidate selected SWA in a different full-fine-tuned CoAtNet run, but it is not the main demonstrated gap
8. **For every >.95 claim, still missing:** exact version/weight hash, one-epoch vs SWA/EMA, TTA count, teacher/fold memberships, all target generation rules, excluded participants, public-score receipt, full-test runtime and distribution rights. Treat author's comments as motivation until those exist
