# One compact MRI-only challenger: native-cache CoAtNet study bags

14 October 2026. Research specification, not an executed experiment.

**Revision N3, 15 October 2026, 08:08 UTC.** This revision makes the existing zero-backbone-dropout choice operational: the exact author's implementation has sixteen relative-position MLP dropout modules that ordinary `drop_rate=0` / `drop_path_rate=0` arguments leave active. Disable them within the encoder only and verify the module inventory before fitting. It also binds `soft_targets=True` for the already-declared fractional fitting targets and `False` for binary outer evaluation; the dataset default otherwise rejects supervised fractional values. Revision N2's channel normalization, mean and standard deviation `(0.5,0.5,0.5)`, remains in force. Both revisions and their evidence are retained in the dated history below. This remains an unexecuted proposal.

## Decision in one paragraph

Define **one new own recipe, Native-CoAtNet-Bag12**, as a directly deployable alternative to the strongest eligible completed native model. Start the CoAtNet trunk from the named public ImageNet initialization, train a new twelve-finding joint multiple-instance head, and fully fine-tune on the existing **2,247 fitting studies** with the current accepted soft targets. Reuse the successful native input pipeline: at most six selected series and sixteen selected grayscale slices per series at 384 pixels. Train on twelve sampled within-series triplets; evaluate one raw epoch-16 checkpoint using every valid triplet, at most 84. Keep the existing **1,156-study outer endpoint**, its labels, masks and leakage groups unchanged. No image teacher, distillation, imported MRI checkpoint, new dataset cache or Gold58 selection is required.

This is a budget-aware Raptor-inspired challenger, **not a reproduction of Raptor's .924 or anyone's .95 recipe**. Its outcome is unknown. Full anatomical coverage, training-time speed, memory fit and competition-runtime compliance have not been established.

Its completed bundled result, .878287 internal macro, trails the native-public .899784 reference, without proving which component caused that difference. The native selector can omit useful complementary series, and sixteen samples can miss focal abnormalities. Reuse avoids an additional unproven selector/crop intervention; it does not establish complete knee coverage.

### Exact training sampler and missing-series rule

Let M be the number of valid same-series windows. Construct the candidate list in stable selected-series order, then increasing start index. **Use series-stratified sampling without replacement:** first choose `min(2, number_of_candidates_in_series)` windows uniformly from every nonempty selected series; then fill the remaining places, up to `min(12,M)` total, by uniform sampling without replacement from the union of all still-unselected candidates. At most six series means the initial allocation never exceeds twelve. No repeated centers, synthetic windows or target-dependent selection.

Seed all draws deterministically from `(run_seed, epoch, study_uid, sampler_version)`, using a stable recorded hash-to-seed convention and a pinned CPU RNG implementation; save RNG/sampler state for resume. Apply the per-series draws in canonical series order. Missing or too-short series contribute no candidates and their unused places pass to the common fill pool. With fewer than twelve total candidates, use every candidate and mask any batch padding. A study with zero candidates is an admission failure: do not silently remove it or substitute zeros.

**Why this one sampler:** a complete study gets two windows from each of its six selected series, retaining anatomical/sequence coverage at the same twelve-encode budget. Pure uniform sampling over 84 candidates would omit any particular 14-window series with probability `C(70,12)/C(84,12) = 9.4155%`, or about .565 series omitted per bag in expectation. It would also allocate more probability to series with more eligible windows. These are sampling calculations, not measured accuracy evidence. Stratification intentionally gives short but nonempty series more weight per candidate; shortages are redistributed without duplication. It does not guarantee lesion capture, and the inference bag still contains all valid windows. This is an own sampling choice, not an assertion that released Raptor stratifies by series. There is no second sampler arm.

**Sampling and pooling limitation.** This coverage choice is not an unbiased approximation to all-window inference. With candidate counts `[14,14,14,14,14,2]` and equal attention scores, training gives the short series `2/12 = 1/6` of pooling mass, while full inference gives it `2/72 = 1/36`. That sixfold shift is exact; its prevalence and effect on accuracy are unknown. Missing whole series alone does not create this unequal-length effect when all remaining series have the same candidate count. Uniform sampling would equalize candidate inclusion, but still has finite-sample softmax-ratio bias and, for six complete series, omits at least one series in **49.276%** of training bags. Retain stratification as the declared coverage tradeoff rather than claiming proven superiority or introducing another fit.

Even at equal series lengths, dense inference changes the attention denominator. A single witness with attention score `log(11)` above all background windows receives mass `.5` in a twelve-window bag containing it, versus `11/(11+83) = .1170` among 84 windows if no additional witnesses appear. This is a conditional synthetic counterexample, not an estimated lesion miss rate or evidence that dense inference is worse. Additional windows can reveal missed findings; prior public dense-inference gains remain counterevidence to inevitable harm. [Focused stress test](rsna-compact-recipe-stress-test-2026-10-14.md)

At inference, use **all M candidates once**, in canonical order, with no random sampling. Both training and inference attention softmax excludes every invalid/padded window; neither pools zeros as anatomy.

## 3. Model, initialization and normalization state

**Initialization contract.** Load only the exact official ImageNet-12k→1k trunk named above, remove its 1,000-class classifier and initialize a new twelve-finding head with run seed **20260922**. Pin the publisher revision, actual weight SHA-256, model configuration and software versions before any future run. Do not substitute an untagged CoAtNet or another checkpoint when resolution fails. A matching numeric seed across architectures does not create identical initial parameters.

No Raptor/nartaa MRI-trained state, all-4,349 model, OAI weights, MRI teacher, model soup, optimizer state or previous own MRI fit enters initialization. Public terms were read; the weight artifact has **not** been downloaded, admitted or inspected. The Apache-2.0 card declaration is source-specific evidence, not a complete competition/prize or downstream-rights clearance.

**One shared encoder and one joint MIL head.** For each triplet obtain its 1,024-dimensional spatially averaged CoAtNet embedding. Let `h[k]` be LayerNorm of that vector. The proposed attention network is `Linear(1024,256) → Tanh → Linear(256,12)`. For each finding, softmax its scores across valid windows only; sum the full 1,024-dimensional `h[k]` vectors with those attention weights. Apply dropout .2 to the resulting twelve pooled vectors in training, then a distinct 1,024-dimensional linear classifier and bias per finding. Output twelve study logits. Use PyTorch's standard initialization for the new linear/LayerNorm modules under the fixed seed; record the resulting initial head hash.

All trunk and head parameters are trainable. Set backbone dropout and stochastic-depth rate to zero; no LoRA, auxiliary losses, metadata embeddings, severity/extent heads or target-specific sampling. This head specification is an own implementation choice where exact released internals were not re-inspected. It preserves the disclosed full-dimensional, finding-specific bag pooling concept without claiming checkpoint compatibility.

**Zero-backbone-dropout implementation contract.** Construct the encoder with `drop_rate=0.0` and `drop_path_rate=0.0`. After loading its admitted state, and before attaching the separate MIL head, recursively set `p=0.0` on every `torch.nn.Dropout` inside **the encoder only**. Do not walk the completed encoder-plus-MIL wrapper: its pooled-vector dropout must remain `.2`. The exact `coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384` factory resolves `coatnet_rmlp_2_rw`, with block types `(C,C,T,T)`, depths `(2,6,14,2)` and `rel_pos_type='mlp'`. That source configuration creates sixteen `RelPosMlp` instances; each hard-codes `drop=(0.125,0.)`, with the nonzero component exposed as `attn.rel_pos.mlp.drop1`. Constructor-level dropout arguments do not override it. Disable these modules to implement the already-declared zero-dropout recipe; this is a deliberate regularization choice, not evidence that zero dropout improves accuracy. Preserve train-mode BatchNorm rather than calling `encoder.eval()` as a dropout workaround. Reapply and assert this construction-time policy on fresh initialization, resume and deployment, because dropout probabilities are not checkpoint tensor values. [Exact model configuration and factory](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models/blob/78c3724f4545c0067d0b4d2f7df6337196be01ce/timm/models/maxxvit.py), [relative-position MLP](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models/blob/78c3724f4545c0067d0b4d2f7df6337196be01ce/timm/layers/pos_embed_rel.py#L344-L395), [MLP dropout modules](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models/blob/78c3724f4545c0067d0b4d2f7df6337196be01ce/timm/layers/mlp.py#L14-L54)

**BatchNorm policy is explicit.** CoAtNet's convolutional BatchNorm layers are in train mode during fitting: affine parameters train, running moments update once per study-bag forward using the valid window batch. Preserve the admitted architecture's BatchNorm epsilon/momentum. Gradient accumulation across eight studies does not make this an eight-study BatchNorm batch: windows within one study are correlated. When M is below twelve, BatchNorm sees only those M valid windows, never padding; its running-state update still occurs once per study bag. Preflight must exercise the smallest admitted M, including M=1 if present, and reject unsupported normalization shapes rather than duplicate data. Use no activation checkpointing in this recipe, so recomputation cannot accidentally update moments twice. At evaluation/deployment, put the entire model in eval mode and use the saved running statistics. No BN replay, post-hoc recalibration or test-set statistic adaptation.

The loss is ordinary masked **BCE-with-logits against fractional study targets**: sum `mask × BCE(logit,target)` over the effective batch and twelve targets, divided by the number of active cells. Positive-class weights are **one for every target**. Preserve .5 as the current recipe's actual soft target; do not infer a new missingness mask or confidence weight from it. No hard thresholding, focal loss, label smoothing, per-window BCE or extra teacher term. This intentionally does not copy Raptor's class-weighted BCE. A positive study label supervises the pooled bag, not every possibly normal triplet.

Output order is fixed: ACL; MCL; Medial Meniscus; Lateral Meniscus; Medial OA; Lateral OA; PF OA; Effusion; Synovitis; Baker's; Contusion; Fracture. [Canonical target schema](https://github.com/GodlyDonuts/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/blob/8300cfd20c2acaeef9d684a81dbc7fe977282a62/rsna/schema.py)

**Proposed schedule, frozen before outcome inspection:**

- One seed, **16 complete epochs**; visit each of the 2,247 fitting studies once per epoch in a seeded shuffled order; no replacement study sampling
- One training GPU, one study per forward, gradient accumulation over eight studies; the last update of each epoch uses seven studies and its actual active-cell denominator
- **281 optimizer updates per epoch; 4,496 total**, with up to 35,952 study bags and 431,424 encoded training windows, excluding engineering checks and replay
- AdamW: peak trunk LR 3e-5; peak head LR 1e-3; weight decay .02 on both groups; betas (.9,.999), epsilon 1e-8
- OneCycleLR over those 4,496 optimizer updates, `pct_start=.15`, cosine annealing, `div_factor=25`, `final_div_factor=10000`, `three_phase=False`, `cycle_momentum=False`; step once after each successful optimizer update
- FP32 model parameters/optimizer state and BF16 autocast for encoder computation on a **BF16-capable training GPU**; attention softmax, pooling and loss in FP32; no BF16-as-T4 assumption
- Global FP32 gradient-norm clip at 3; finite input/logit/loss/gradient checks; nonfinite updates stop and remain a numerical failure, not a license to change precision/LR after viewing accuracy
- No spatial/intensity augmentation, TTA or additional slice-selection randomness beyond the specified bag/study sampling; no EMA/SWA or best-epoch selection

The chosen hardware must pass memory and recovery admission with this exact single-study, twelve-window graph. If it cannot, this recipe is blocked; do not silently create smaller-backbone, smaller-resolution or different-batch variants. Fixes to a verified implementation defect are distinguishable from changing the scientific recipe and require a new bound execution record.

## 6. Staged admission and runtime gates

**Before committing the production fit:** pass gates 1, 2, 3 and 5, and the hardware-matched feasibility estimate in gate 6. Reserve time/storage for final qualification. Initial-state timing and numerical probes establish engineering feasibility only. **After fitting:** qualify the actual final state under gate 4 and repeat the complete offline checks in gate 6 before the single primary outer endpoint and deployment. Final-weight agreement cannot be certified before those weights exist.

1. **Information/provenance gate.** Bind exact fit/outer rosters, leakage graph, accepted targets/masks, cache and code revisions. Prove no protected, reserved, quarantine or outer component enters fitting. Exclude OAI/all-4,349 trained weights and any inherited MRI-fit state. Review exact initialization and copied-code terms separately from current-label terms; no new legal acceptance, weight intake or publication is implied here.
2. **Input adapter gate.** First resolve the pinned initialization configuration and require channel mean/std `(0.5,0.5,0.5)` and input size `(3,384,384)`. Stop on a missing or mismatched configuration; do not silently choose another preset. Before any clinical fixture, exercise training and inference preprocessing on synthetic FP32 inputs: black `0` must map to `-1`, midpoint `0.5` to `0`, and white `1` to `1` in every channel, with maximum absolute error at most `1e-6`. Include ramps and distinct channel-coded patterns to verify `2*x-1`, ordering, exactly one normalization and train/inference parity. Hold all other preprocessing identical. These checks establish transform consistency, not MRI accuracy or AUC. On a future explicitly admitted fitting-only fixture, verify native tensor parity before triplet assembly, exact selected index/channel ordering, masks, finite ranges, no cross-series windows and no lost study. Audit the number of valid windows and short-series cases. A 6×16 setting alone is not proof that every study has 84 windows. Test missing series, fewer than three samples, repeated/invalid positions and decode failures without silent schema repair.
3. **GPU and recovery gate.** Before model computation, record an instantiated-model stochastic-module inventory: the source-derived expected relative-position paths are `stages.2.blocks.{0..13}.attn.rel_pos.mlp.drop1` and `stages.3.blocks.{0..1}.attn.rel_pos.mlp.drop1`; all sixteen and every other trunk dropout must have `p=0`, stochastic depth must be inactive, and only the separately declared MIL pooled-vector dropout may have `p=.2`. Assert this after reconstruction/resume too; an unexpected module or positive trunk dropout probability blocks admission. This inventory is a required future check, not a check already executed. Verify all intended trunk/head parameters receive finite gradients and change; validate the real twelve-window graph, actual peak VRAM, BatchNorm buffer behavior, exact update count and continuous-versus-resumed state/prediction agreement. Recovery must include optimizer, scheduler, sampler, RNG and BN buffers. Synthetic arithmetic or a tiny forward alone does not pass this gate.
4. **Post-fit numerical deployment gate.** Compare the final trained state in deployment FP16 against the same FP32 state on a predeclared representative fitting-only qualification roster and numerical fixtures, covering missing/short inputs and all twelve outputs. Preserve any stricter existing project criterion; proposed maximum absolute probability difference is **.003**, plus finite/order-complete output checks and chunk-size invariance within the predeclared tolerance. A prior native full-FP16 attempt failed .003 and is not evidence this candidate passes. Do not widen the bound after inspection. Failed precision qualification blocks the proposed deployment path; any alternative must be separately budgeted and qualified before outer comparison.
5. **Storage gate.** Reuse native cache bytes in place; materialize only in-memory study/window tensors. No second whole-dataset cache and no new raw MRI staging. Preserve the reported shared-storage floor of **20 GiB and 5,000 free files** throughout atomic writes and recovery. Parameter/optimizer tensors alone suggest roughly .3 GB per final model and .9 GB per full FP32 AdamW restart state, not a measured package size. Plan no more than two rolling restart states plus the final inference state; budget the temporary atomic-write copy, original init, dependencies and logs. A provisional **4-GiB new-artifact envelope** must be checked against actual bytes and the free-space floor before admission. Do not keep sixteen full optimizer snapshots or delete unrelated artifacts to fit. If native cache access is unavailable, bounded streaming of the **same admitted bytes** needs its own staging/I/O budget and authorization; do not automatically switch to source geometry or rebuild a corpus.
6. **Pre-fit feasibility estimate; post-fit offline qualification.** Before the fit, pin Python, torch, timm, CUDA and needed wheels, and benchmark the proposed graph and data path on the target hardware to estimate the complete delivery budget. After fitting, repeat the full path with the bound final state; disable network and remote-code/weight fallback; bind checkpoint and code hashes. Exercise the actual competition input-to-output route, decode behavior, packaging, process restart, two-device assembly and exact output schema. Benchmark representative full-scale input volume on the target hardware. Include cold start, loading, decoding/preprocessing, transfer, inference and output writing. No three-example notebook or submission-to-score turnaround proves the nine-hour limit.

**Compute planning, not a throughput forecast.** The model-card count gives at most 12×47.7 = **572.4 GMAC per training-study encoder forward** and 84×47.7 = **4,006.8 GMAC per inference study**. These exclude backward, data movement, preprocessing and the new head; the source count also includes a classifier removed here. The full 1,156-study outer pass has at most 97,104 window encodings. Missing series reduce work. GMACs cannot predict wall time from an RTX4090/H100/T4 label alone.

Measure a warm steady-state training interval and the full I/O path, then budget fitting, outer evaluation, possible final refit, offline qualification and recovery **before** reserving the run. If the authorized scale cannot establish compliance, runtime remains unqualified. The exact hidden population is not known from the visible examples.

## 7. Decision rule and stopping condition

Commit the one production fit only if the pre-fit provenance, native-input, GPU/recovery and storage gates pass and measured feasibility leaves enough time for evaluation, final-state qualification and delivery. After fitting, require final-state precision and end-to-end offline qualification before the primary outer endpoint or deployment. A meaningful full-twelve-target improvement with acceptable sparse-support uncertainty and proven deployment feasibility can advance it directly. A tie favors the already-qualified simpler/cheaper artifact. An uncertain rare-target-only gain is not automatic promotion, and a worse result does not justify a backbone or cache sweep.

Keep all already-paid completed evidence in the decision. If this single candidate cannot be admitted or finished with time left for deployment, leave its experiment slot unused and retain the strongest eligible completed model. The fixed endpoint does not require a second seed, image-teacher construction, new selector, mechanistic controls or a larger architecture search before deciding which final single model to use.

**Superseded 14 October choice:** channel mean `(0.485,0.456,0.406)` and standard deviation `(0.229,0.224,0.225)`. Those values were borrowed from the previously audited MRI-trained Raptor inference contract. They do not match the generic ImageNet initialization selected for this new recipe.

**Verified source contract:** the exact checkpoint's [original configuration addition, commit ee3938f907f7cbee730c05c5303034513bad6010](https://huggingface.co/timm/coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384.sw_in12k_ft_in1k/commit/ee3938f907f7cbee730c05c5303034513bad6010) specifies mean/std `0.5` in all channels, fixed `3×384×384` input, bicubic interpolation, crop percentage `1.0`, squash crop mode and average global pooling. Revision N2 aligns channel standardization with that initialization. It preserves native intensity scaling, physically aspect-preserving rendering and the declared bilinear resize; the natural-image crop/resize metadata does not justify introducing a new MRI crop or geometry transform. All other recipe and endpoint choices remain unchanged. No two-fit normalization search is required to correct this source-contract mismatch.

Synthetic arithmetic confirms the conventions are different: a constant raw triplet at `0.5` becomes `[0,0,0]` under the corrected transform, versus approximately `[0.0655,0.1964,0.4178]` under the superseded transform. This does not establish that fine-tuning could not accommodate the old choice or that the correction improves AUC. The future adapter must still pass gate 2; no model, clinical-data, training or inference test was performed for this revision. Passing this gate advances only to the existing feasibility checks. This correction concerns the unexecuted CoAtNet challenger and establishes no defect in the current DINO model.
