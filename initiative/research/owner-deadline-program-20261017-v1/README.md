## Corrections that affect the plan

1. **A head fold is not a held-out encoder fold.** The released CoAtNet was
   developed on this cohort and the S6 all-data encoder was trained on 4,263
   report-labelled studies. Splitting their cached features into five head folds
   does not manufacture strict OOF predictions for the whole model. Such a
   result may describe weak-label agreement, but cannot select blend weights as
   independent OOF. A clean claim requires source encoders that excluded each
   evaluated component or a genuinely untouched admitted cohort. Gold58 is not
   promoted into a tuning endpoint.
2. **A new pooling mechanism must be new relative to our completed controls.**
   Context, spatial regional/global-repeat, confidence and pair-ranking heads
   already fitted. A geometry/query head must introduce explicit admitted
   plane/protocol/position information and compare against those controls.
   Slots and normalized within-series position are not proof of anatomical
   registration between sequences. Inspect the actual cached metadata before
   promising a physical-coordinate experiment.
3. **Teacher agreement is not truth or calibrated confidence.** Two teachers
   may share supervision and errors. Their raw probability gap can reflect
   calibration. First measure agreement, changed pair order and source exposure
   on approved fitting data. Freeze any gate before fitting its matched control;
   do not infer improved disease accuracy from teacher fidelity.
4. **The resource schedule must use actual session limits and elapsed time.**
   Our first all-94 epoch took 12,199 seconds; the Kaggle account currently allows
   two batch GPU sessions. Paper runtime estimates and apparent spare submission
   slots do not create another GPU lane. The current S6 notebook executes the
   complete CoAtNet path even at W_A=0, so its budget must include both models. Preserve accepted numerical continuity
   and the ready learned-E1 inference priority. The source-DICOM crop proposal
   needs actual decoding, localizer, tensor and throughput acceptance; cropping
   the old resized cache cannot recreate it.
5. **Reported public scores remain attributed.** Our best independent N3 is
   0.894; our reproduced external parent is 0.949. Context, regional and full-field
   variants are 0.948. The S6 source audit is not our 0.950 result, and an ensemble
   reported at 0.956 is not an independently reproduced private-generalization
   result. The paper itself does not establish 0.97.

## Concrete next sequence

- Preserve and score the already completed whole-parent E1 under its existing
  finite owner; finish fixed E3 and expanded N3 without restarting healthy work.
- Verify S6's exact checkpoint, anatomy and full source tree against its manifests,
  reuse existing identical assets, then run unchanged offline inference with
  actual two-T4 time/RAM/output evidence. Do not silently remove its capped
  unreadable-study fallback or mistake that fallback for a pure single-lineage
  prediction. Admit one distinctly identified public comparison after that pass.
- Only after S6 works, measure complementarity with the CoAtNet on a cohort whose
  source exposure is stated. A fixed equal-rank diagnostic blend is a separate
  experiment, not a claim of independent OOF selection.
- Choose one new adaptation mechanism from measured differences. Dual-teacher
  gating and geometry/query residuals are candidates, not simultaneous changes
  to a running recipe. Source-DICOM local/global representation remains a
  distinct compute-funded comparison; the present crop-only inference negative
  does not answer retraining with physical crops.
