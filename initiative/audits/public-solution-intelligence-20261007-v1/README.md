## What their actual recipes reveal

1. **The shared DINOv2 baseline is an ensemble.** The downloaded Pilkwang manifest contains20 members with five-fold identities and a common recipe: DINOv2-small,336pixels,12 selected slices, three-slice channels,130mm field of view,20–80% slice band, six anatomy/contrast/fat-suppression slots, CLS-plus-mean-patch pooling and last-six-block fine-tuning. These are actual manifest fields, not an inference from model names.
2. **Training duration is longer for at least one original member.** Safe decoding of `m_e5427d6c21.pt` exposes a30-epoch schedule/10-epoch cycles and stored epoch25. Its stored encoder/head learning rates are8e-6/1e-3, batch8 and weight decay0.02. That differs from our fixed-ten-epoch rank32 recipe, especially its1e-4 encoder LR. This is a concrete hypothesis about adaptation strength and duration, not proof that longer training or a lower LR will improve our model.
3. **Small DINOv3 models are substantially task-adapted.** The five `m_f*.pt` checkpoint configs specify `vit_small_patch16_dinov3.lvd1689m`,336pixels,16 slices, token conditioning and `xcodex` pooling. Tensor graphs were actually decoded. Larger base size alone does not reproduce these task-specific representations.
4. **Physical input construction differs.** Baseline/source code specifies130mm cropping, geometry-based laterality and ordering, contrast-aware series slots, three-slice channels, per-target slot attention and repeated window inference. These need a numerical comparison against our current MRI tensors; native384 resolution alone does not establish equivalent anatomy coverage or scale.
5. **Supervision differs, beyond the model.** The attached training source loads graded target/confidence columns, applies confidence weights and can fall back to a multilingual report lexicon. One source uses weight `0.25 + 0.75*confidence`, while our accepted dataset masks unmentioned targets. Source also contains annotated-label paths and model/mixture selection against public annotations. We captured source/manifests but did not inspect protected annotation rows, fit labels, or claim their evaluation is compatible with ours. The currently running label-source experiment addresses only one part of this difference.
6. **High-score wrappers share much ancestry.** The original three linked notebooks attach common DINO, CoAtNet/Raptor and RadImageNet families. The reproduction bundle documents41 unique checkpoint members and a staged DINOv2→DINOv3→RadImageNet→CoAtNet/Raptor prediction pipeline. Multiple differently named wrappers are not multiple independent discoveries or independent teachers.
7. **Inference details matter.** Bundled reproduction scripts and the fast2×T4 notebook provide offline weight discovery, renderer checks, complete member use, rank fusion and target-specific blends. Output ranks of1/3,2/3,1 on three examples come from cohort ranking; they do not establish disease probabilities or explain our low accuracy by themselves.

Primary sources captured include [Pilkwang baseline](https://www.kaggle.com/code/pilkwang/rsna-knee-baseline-v1), [D4-lite](https://www.kaggle.com/code/pjmathematician/rsna-knee-d4-lite), [Amanatar](https://www.kaggle.com/code/amanatar/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection), [E11 source](https://www.kaggle.com/code/sofiaanjenje/rsna-knee-e11-train), [fast2×T4 inference](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jiweiliu/rsna-knee-fast-2xt4-inference), and [reproduction assets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tonylica/rsna-knee-bend-dinov3-0917-repro-assets). ## Research consequence

Prioritize a faithful component reproduction and a fitting-only input/label/representation comparison. Separate one strong DINO member from ensemble effects, bind its actual preprocessing and checkpoint fingerprints, then compare anatomy scale, slot occupancy, graded supervision and encoder adaptation against ours. Treat published OOF/training membership as unverified until independently established; do not automatically relabel, distill onto our development boundary, or promote from a score in a title. Public model/source collection is now complete for this bounded dependency graph; execution parity and a demonstrated improvement remain unfinished. The existing healthy ten-epoch comparisons continue unchanged.
