# Successor modeling strategy for UCF Newton

Authored independently by `/root/competition_contract`, September 22, 2026. Medical encoder facts were separately checked by `/root/competition_contract/medical_encoder_sources`. Planning only: no weights/data downloaded, agreements accepted, jobs submitted or compute purchased. The earlier strategy and its review remain preserved.

**Use the larger resources to compare stronger representations, recover useful spatial detail and test MRI adaptation—not to assume that the largest network wins.** My first high-compute candidate is a DINOv3-L/16 image encoder with a small sequence-aware study head, compared directly with DINOv2-L/14 with registers. Keep ConvNeXt-Tiny as the inexpensive reproducibility control and potential deployment student. Add one medical encoder and one genuine 3D challenger after the initial screen. This is a proposed ranking of experiments, not an established ranking of knee models.

The accepted data still contain 3,403 training studies with observed labels, 21,044 observed training cells, and only seven MCL, nine Baker's and five fracture positives; synovitis has fifteen negatives. More GPUs do not create missing independent examples. The 441-study weak-validation set and 58 historically exposed gold studies retain all existing restrictions. This memo inherits the coordinator's current official Kaggle contract: mean twelve AUCs, approximately 1,300 hidden studies without reports, no internet and nine-hour final inference. It does not recheck those rules or establish external-model eligibility.

## What Newton changes

UCF's official inventory lists 29 dual-H100 80-GB PCIe nodes, four eight-H100 nodes, and V100 nodes with smaller memory, connected by InfiniBand. These are cluster resources, not our assigned allocation. Verify each granted node's actual GPU, memory, topology, driver and job limits. Avoid treating PCIe devices as an assumed NVLink domain. The published scheduler uses Slurm, shared PI allocations and fairshare; remaining GPU-hours and queue availability must be observed rather than inferred from the public monthly allowance. [Hardware](https://arcc.ist.ucf.edu/index.php/resources/newton/about-newton), [allocation guide](https://arcc.ist.ucf.edu/docs/scheduler/), [queue policy](https://arcc.ist.ucf.edu/docs/scheduler/limitations/).

Make one two-GPU node the preferred scheduling unit. Initially run independent folds, seeds or feature extractors on its GPUs; compare that throughput with two-GPU distributed training for a model that benefits from a larger batch. Additional nodes run independent jobs first. Use multi-node SSL only when a short same-workload scaling test shows worthwhile wall-time improvement after communication, I/O and queue costs. InfiniBand availability alone does not justify a coupled multi-node job.

Use the CPU cluster for verified DICOM decoding, geometry-aware native-resolution transforms, sharding, checksums and bootstrap evaluation. Preserve source identities and all technical exclusions. Throttle file-system concurrency; pack data into indexed shards instead of generating millions of slice files. Stage only what each GPU job needs, then reuse immutable caches. Published Stokes quota/inode and no-backup rules are not proof of our Newton storage entitlement; confirm project storage and maintain recoverable checkpoints elsewhere. [Submission resources](https://arcc.ist.ucf.edu/docs/scheduler/scripts/), [storage policy](https://arcc.ist.ucf.edu/docs/data/files/).

|---|---|---|

Strong established representation comparator, using CLS plus pooled patch features. | Official Apache-2.0 weights; pin exact revision/hash. General-image results do not prove knee transfer. [Model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md) |
| **DINOv3-L/16, web variant** | 300M parameters; distilled from its 7B teacher, trained on LVD-1689M. Gram anchoring targets dense-feature degradation; this makes local features worth testing for subtle findings. | Custom DINOv3 terms, not Apache. Verify exact access and redistribution compatibility first; choose web rather than satellite weights. [Card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10104), [license](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov3/blob/main/LICENSE.md) |
| **SigLIP2 So400m/16** | Objective-diverse comparator trained on WebLI with image/text and self-supervised objectives. Use its image tower; no report input at inference. | Official `google/siglip2-so400m-patch16-384` is Apache-2.0. It is not MRI-specific. [Card](https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-so400m-patch16-384), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14786) |
| **MedSigLIP-448 or BiomedCLIP** | MedSigLIP offers a 400M image encoder exposed to CT/MRI slices; BiomedCLIP offers a smaller 224-pixel ViT-B/16 trained on PMC-15M figure/caption pairs. Test medical-domain transfer rather than assuming it. | MedSigLIP requires Health AI Developer Foundations terms; BiomedCLIP's Microsoft card is MIT. Neither establishes this twelve-label knee task. [MedSigLIP](https://huggingface.co/google/medsiglip-448), [terms](https://developers.google.com/health-ai-developer-foundations/terms), [BiomedCLIP](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224) |
| **VoCo-Base SSL** | Available 53M 3D Swin encoder checkpoint, `VoCo_B_SSL_head.pt`; geometric pretraining on PreCT-160K. A practical generic 3D comparator. | Author code/weights Apache-2.0; CT-to-knee-MRI is a substantial domain shift. Verify matched encoder keys, not merely a successful permissive load. [Author release](https://github.com/Luffy03/Large-Scale-Medical), [weights](https://huggingface.co/Luffy503/VoCo) |

DINOv3 is newer and improves general visual benchmarks; that is a reason to run a matched comparison, not to discard DINOv2. At 224 pixels, both have legal patch dimensions but different token counts. Compare identical anatomy, pixels, selected slices and head capacity, and report compute/token differences. Later use a common native-derived 448-pixel field: divisible by both fourteen and sixteen. Do not silently crop unsupported sizes. A separate native-default-resolution comparison is allowed for medical/SigLIP encoders, clearly labeled as changing resolution too.

Do not start by fine-tuning the 6.7B DINOv3 teacher. Its weight/optimizer/activation footprint and deployment cost are disproportionate to this labeled cohort. If smaller models plateau and access terms permit, a frozen large teacher can be a bounded distillation experiment; its student still requires an upstream provenance/license assessment. Published model size is not a success criterion.

Two recent knee-specific candidates deserve an early **eligibility/provenance review**, then a frozen-feature screen if cleared:

- **OrthoFoundation-L:** the paper links the author repository, which now exposes `OrthoFoudation-L.pth` as a 1.13-GB LFS artifact. Current README describes 1,251,655 knee radiographs/MRI slices from OAI, fastMRI and a restricted private cohort. It continues DINOv3-L pretraining. The project says MIT, but that does not remove upstream DINOv3 obligations. The January preprint and current README describe different task counts; pin the actual version. Public checkpoint presence does not prove source-overlap clearance or reproducibility. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18250), [author repository](https://github.com/ytrsk/OrthoFoundation).
- **OrthoDiffusion:** three orientation-specific 3D backbones pretrained on 15,948 knee examinations. Official code is public; a paper-linked HF card lists three 553-MB checkpoints and MIT terms. Confirm release linkage and exact bytes before use. Private pretraining data are restricted; prior surgery/hardware and acute complex fractures were excluded, so transfer to those competition cases needs explicit stress testing. Extract fixed-timestep features instead of running a generative sampling chain; do not import its EHR branch. Its eight knee targets differ from our twelve definitions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.20752v1), [code](https://github.com/lt-0123/OrthoDiffusion), [weight listing](https://huggingface.co/lanstat0123/orthodiffusion/tree/main).

Neither private pretraining data nor public weights alone settle Kaggle eligibility. Check the host's accessibility/cost requirements and required winner redistribution against each exact artifact. Do not acquire private clinical datasets by assuming the paper grants access. KneeCoT and derivatives remain excluded under the existing contract.

The separately authored [medical-source fact sheet](medical-encoder-source-facts.md) identifies useful limits: **3DINO-ViT** has gated CC BY-NC-ND terms and explicit no-adaptation language, so it is not a routine fine-tuning dependency; **Decipher-MR** includes knee MRI but this review found no confirmed public checkpoint/license; **MedImageInsight** has an official medical model card but no verified Microsoft-origin offline weight route in this bounded check. Keep these research references conditional, not executable baseline promises.

## Architecture and MRI adaptation

Start with independent 2D series encoding, using same-series physical slice positions and valid spacing, followed by slice-to-series and series-to-study attention. Include plane, fluid sensitivity and fat suppression separately, with unknown values and missing-series masks. Use twelve target queries, plus a plain max/mean baseline. Compare CLS-only embeddings with CLS plus pooled patch features; only then add a small local patch-attention module. Avoid all-to-all attention over every patch in an entire examination. Attention is not validated lesion localization.

Rotate training coverage across all eligible series; compare 16 versus 32 slices per series and six-series sampling versus fuller coverage. Increase coverage before concluding a focal target needs a larger encoder. The 2.5D challenger uses neighboring slices from the same series at documented physical offsets. Never stack unregistered planes as aligned channels. The 3D branch processes individual anisotropic series or slabs, then uses the same study fusion. Preserve through-plane spacing; interpolation does not create missing native detail. Evaluate pretrained VoCo or eligible OrthoDiffusion against both a compact scratch 3D control and the selected 2D model, matching image coverage and separately measuring compute.

Use training-fold MRI continuation as the main high-compute representation experiment. Compare: (a) untouched initialization; (b) DINO/iBOT-style self-distillation and masked-feature prediction; (c) one reconstruction-based SSL alternative at matched encoder, data and update budget. Choose a single adaptation schedule from a short pilot rather than reconstructing billion-image foundation pretraining. Monitor collapse, representation drift and downstream ranking. Same-series crops must retain overlapping anatomy; arbitrary slices from different locations are not guaranteed semantic positives. Add cross-sequence consistency only after valid series correspondence and retain sequence-specific information. [SB-SSL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.13923) supplies limited-data knee precedent; [FOMO25](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11679) shows task-dependent MRI SSL benefits and unreliable gains from simply increasing size or duration. Neither guarantees this competition's outcome.

Every learned adaptation is fold-specific: exclude inner held-out groups, weak validation and gold even when no labels are used. A single SSL encoder trained across all training folds would leak evaluation images into inner-CV representation fitting. Fixed unfitted public encoders may be cached across folds. After architecture selection, train the final encoder on permitted training groups only. Technically eligible training images without observed labels may support SSL, but held correspondence cannot support report pairing or invented diagnoses. A report contrastive auxiliary task remains lower priority; use verified training pairs only and account for template/language shortcuts and false-negative report pairs.

Retain per-target normalized masked BCE and the capped balancing ablation. Multi-GPU loss must normalize observed counts across ranks; verify accumulation semantics rather than assuming microbatch target means equal the intended full-batch loss. Masks, negatives, semantic holds and quarantine remain authoritative. Compare unweighted loss, group-balanced sampling and capped square-root weighting separately. Keep rare-study exposure counts. Pseudo-labels cannot manufacture trustworthy fracture positives; if attempted later, they require a separate policy, fold-local teachers and strict disagreement/hold rules. A targeted, blinded training-image annotation project could be more valuable than another very large encoder for rare targets, but no such labels or expert arrangements are assumed here.

## Ordered experiment contract

| Stage | Question and controlled experiment | Stop/advance rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Hardware/loader/resume pilot; same batch on one and two allocated GPUs; cold Kaggle-like decode test | Reproducible masks/resume, bounded memory and measured throughput; fix bottlenecks before scale. |
| 1 | Frozen DINOv2-L versus DINOv3-L; ConvNeXt control; one medical encoder, then SigLIP2 if useful | Same folds/head/coverage; select at most two representations using all twelve per-target results and within-fold ranking, not one rare case. Conditional knee checkpoints enter only after eligibility review. |
| 2 | Final-block versus full fine-tuning; denser slices; 2.5D; target attention | One factor per comparison. Advance repeatable within-fold ranking benefit or demonstrable complementary predictions at acceptable runtime. |
| 3 | Fold-local MRI SSL continuation versus untouched initialization; one reconstruction alternative | Equal data/update budgets; gain must survive seed repetition and cohort stress tests. Stop on collapse or no useful downstream change. |
| 4 | Native-derived 448-pixel detail and one genuine 3D branch | Distinguish resolution/coverage gains from architecture gains. Avoid multiplying all resolution, encoder and loss combinations. |
| 5 | Accepted supervision versus reduced corrected-cell supervision, then equal-observed-cell comparisons | Fixed validation masks, seeds and exposure; report coverage and rare-example sensitivity. No legacy-label or quarantine bypass. |
| 6 | Replicate finalists; distill if needed; compact ensemble and cold offline rehearsal | Model selection frozen before final gold diagnostic; final package comfortably fits the nine-hour bound. |

Keep three predeclared related-group development folds and two development seeds. Use a fixed epoch schedule or choose stopping/hyperparameters only inside each fold's training portion; never select the checkpoint on the fold used for its reported comparison. For each target, aggregate within-fold AUC using positive–negative pair counts as weights; this avoids ranking scores produced by separately fitted fold models against each other. Report each fold, pair counts, undefined folds and all twelve targets; compute the full twelve-target macro only when all targets have valid within-fold pairs. Pooled OOF AUC is secondary because cross-fold score scales can differ. Use paired group-bootstrap differences within fixed folds, disclose invalid resamples and call these intervals conditional on fitted models; report seed variation separately. Only five distinct fracture-positive leakage components exist; these are not verified patient identities and cannot support precise estimates; repeat leave-one-rare-group sensitivity checks. Use the locked 441 weak-validation studies for one predeclared confirmation after inner-fold selection, not repeated tuning; one positive in three targets and three synovitis negatives make aggressive tuning untenable. Gold is historically exposed, evaluation-only, and excluded from architecture/weight/checkpoint/ensemble selection. Scanner/report-style proxies are not known centers, and reports cannot become inference features. More parallel jobs must not turn into an unrecorded validation search.

A gain advances if it repeats across seeds/folds under the predeclared within-fold aggregation, without consistent target collapse, and improves the score/runtime tradeoff. A positive paired interval is stronger evidence, not a cure for few independent positives. If a rare-target gain disappears after removing one group, label it fragile. Predeclare the candidate count and tie-break toward the simpler deployable model when evidence is inconclusive. The final objective remains all twelve AUCs, even if better-supported targets supply more informative early diagnostics.

## Resource-scalable execution and deployment

Express the budget in measured **GPU-hours**, not reserved cluster size or promised calendar days. Let `s` be seconds per supervised study and `g` allocated GPUs for that timed job: twenty epochs over 3,403 studies cost approximately `18.91 × s × g` GPU-hours before validation. Three folds × two seeds at two-thirds training size cost about four such full-run equivalents per candidate. Record the actual training-pool size in each fold. Two GPUs that halve wall time do not halve allocated GPU-hours.

Proposed ceilings, adjustable to the actual shared allocation: pilot ≤8 GPU-hours; frozen screens ≤24; core shortlist/replication ≤128; MRI SSL continuation ≤160; one spatial/3D branch ≤160; final confirmation/distillation/inference reserve ≤120. Total expanded ceiling: 600 GPU-hours, **not** a claim that every matrix cell fits or that 600 hours are available. Release each stage only after pilot estimates and prior evidence; reserve the final 20% rather than spending it on model search. If availability is small, finish the core baseline and deployment branch and omit later stages. No rental cost is incurred or proposed as a substitute for unavailable allocation.

For opportunistic execution, use bounded jobs with atomic checkpoints and exact release/fold/config identities. Save optimizer, scheduler, precision scaler where used, sampler, RNG and progress states; verify restart equivalence. Save before wall-time expiry and handle preemption where the scheduler provides notice. Requeuing/restart behavior must be tested, not assumed. BF16 and activation checkpointing are sensible H100 starting points; different granted hardware may require another precision path. Use FSDP only if a measured memory problem warrants its complexity. Multi-node SSL must beat independent useful jobs on measured throughput and completion risk.

Keep the 82.6-GB accepted cache for the baseline; create any higher-resolution derivative on the cluster with source hashes and a separate manifest. A 1,024-dimensional FP16 vector per 819,635 cached slices is only about 1.68 GB, but retaining every DINOv3 patch at 384 pixels would be about 967 GB before indices. Stream or compress selected features; do not blindly cache all patch tokens or rebuild all native data on the nearly full laptop. Native resolution, paired 2.5D and spatially valid 3D require source-based transforms rather than upsampling the old cache. Confirm peak temporary storage and retain the 20-GiB local floor.

The H100 training server is not the final Kaggle runtime. Rehearse the entire submitted ensemble on the actual permitted Kaggle hardware with internet disabled, including decoding, model load, preprocessing, missing-series handling and CSV generation. Target six hours for roughly 1,300 studies—about 16.6 seconds/study including overhead—and test long-study tails. Use H100 capacity to train teachers if beneficial, then distill/select a base/small encoder or compact 2D+3D blend that meets deployment limits. Fit distillation and blend choices without held-out labels/images leaking into training. Distillation does not waive upstream rights or repair an ineligible teacher.

Begin with an equal-weight blend of two complementary accepted models. Any fitted global blend rule must be chosen inside each outer fold's training portion, then evaluated on that untouched outer portion; fitting and reporting on the same OOF predictions is optimistic. Compare probability/rank blends using this within-fold separation. Preserve inference-compatible transformations; fold-specific rank normalization is not automatically transferable to a final refit. Avoid twelve free blend weights. Monotonic calibration alone cannot improve a model's AUC. Add TTA only after measured benefit and full-runtime accounting. Keep the original five-per-day/two-final submission discipline and freeze the solution before the final deadline. The deliverable from this research phase is a reproducible experiment route; neither available H100s nor published model claims establish a winning score.
