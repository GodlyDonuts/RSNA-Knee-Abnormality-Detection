## Required clarifications to the root proposal

1. **One cell, several reasons.** Exactly52,884 canonical UID×target cells need one row each, but correspondence, uncertainty, semantic disagreement and duplicate-derived holds can overlap. Store an exhaustive reason set plus a deterministic primary display category. Do not force these into mutually exclusive source facts or sum overlapping counts as unique losses. Gold membership and split are a separate axis. The four-state report annotation remains separate from report-supervision eligibility.
2. **Rejected is not resolved.** A rejected proposal can leave a genuine source/rubric disagreement. Retaining the current observed state does not by itself authorize its use. The separate additional003 rejection and additional004 four rejections must remain discoverable as unresolved evidence unless an actual later disposition resolves their reliability. Conversely, two distinct quotations that both adequately support the same state are not automatically a reliability dispute just because their bytes differ. An explicit bounded interpretation must distinguish harmless quote alternatives from missing identity, polarity, temporal, causal or threshold context. A known quote-scope defect cannot be passed by a substring validator.
3. **Do not turn the lexicon into a clearance rule.** Completion of a candidate list proves its membership was accounted for, not that its complement is clinically safe. Residual negative cells and observations outside proxies need a disposition using existing full source-pass evidence, an explicit bounded reuse assessment, or an accurately named incomplete-reliability hold. These routes do not require a blanket new reread. Lexical hits that are deliberately excluded must be called unreviewed retrieval scope, never diagnosed errors. Unassigned cues require explicit report-scope membership; do not invent a target. All-target report holds should be a measured last-resort scope decision rather than an automatic expansion of every cue.
4. **A hold has no automatic expiry.** A new accepted correction does not silently clear a hold that bound the previous state or quotation. It invalidates the affected release binding and requires a new hold disposition or explicit release decision with current evidence. Later matching reader opinions also cannot remove a hold by majority vote or latest-file precedence.
5. **Distinct authors remain distinct actual people/agents, not record counts.** A source freeze, normalization wrapper, later reconsideration and copied archived record by the same author contribute one source-reader identity. An original reviewer preread can legitimately support reuse; an original final reviewed row alone cannot be represented as a second independent source freeze. Unknown session IDs remain null. Reused findings must match exact UID, target, rubric and source and cover the relevant reason for the gate.

Class-collapse checks need an explicit prior acceptance rule, not a threshold chosen after seeing a convenient result. Zero surviving positive or negative classes in an intended supervised target/split must be an explicit release failure or a declared changed learning/evaluation scope. Report severe but nonzero depletion and effective unique-group counts. Numeric coverage cannot prove semantic reliability, and disagreement-based censoring can preferentially remove difficult cases. The later training strategy should measure this selection bias in independent non-gold supervision ablations; no gold fitting or criterion relaxation is justified by low coverage.
