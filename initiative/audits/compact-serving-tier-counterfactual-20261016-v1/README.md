# Selection_Tier-only counterfactual

A metadata-only replay of frozen N3 selection on the exact fitting and outer
populations drops only Selection_Tier. Of 2,247 fitting studies, two have tier-1
series and one changes selected series/order. **None of 1,156 outer studies changes**.
[Exact sanitized result](actual-metadata-only.json).

It reads accepted study/mask/series-provenance metadata, not clinical target values,
images, reports, gold or confirmation. This rules out that single field alone as
the held-out selection-gap explanation. It does not prove equality with every
field in official metadata or parity of pixels, normalization or serving precision.
