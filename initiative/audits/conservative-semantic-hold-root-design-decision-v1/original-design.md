# Proposed final supervision disposition — pending independent assessment

This is a design proposal, not an accepted release policy, annotation change or QA closure. The frozen rubric already requires unresolved findings to stay masked. A release can preserve a disputed annotation while excluding that exact report target from supervised loss; exclusion does not assert that its clinical state is uncertain or that either reader is right.

Use a separate required semantic-supervision registry, rather than manufacturing accepted correction cases. Every hold must identify an exact study UID and target, the original source batch/index/report hash, the current reviewed annotation and active correction if present, the actual unresolved evidence records and their immutable hashes/selectors, and a concrete reason for excluding supervision. Preserve all source, original and effective annotations, reader identities, initial judgments, later reconsiderations and rejections. Never convert a hold into a negative target or a claimed clinical consensus.

The final all-cell ledger must account for every one of the 52,884 report targets exactly once. Distinguish existing unmentioned/uncertain masks, correspondence exclusions, accepted current observations supported by bound reading evidence, and observed cells requiring a semantic hold. Reconcile the fixed cartilage, bone and wording worklists against actual later artifacts. A closed proposal or a mechanically copied record is not a fresh reading. Existing full source passes may be reused with truthful identities and scope; missing historical session IDs stay null.

A conservative hold may cover an unresolved source-supported state disagreement, an unresolved evidence-scope problem, or an explicit incomplete reliability review. A broad lexical match alone is not a clinical error diagnosis. If such a queue is conservatively excluded pending interpretation, name that reason and measure the coverage loss. Do not pretend the queue was reviewed or arbitrarily infer a target for unassigned wording. An all-target hold for an unresolved report-scope ambiguity is more honest than guessed target localization, but requires explicit membership and coverage accounting.

All hold definitions are computed without gold values. Validate complete source/rubric/review/correction hash maps and the exact coverage ledger before applying them. Any changed reviewed artifact, correction case, source text, hold decision or ledger invalidates the relevant final QA binding. Final QA resolutions must explain how each finite worklist was accepted, already masked, or deliberately excluded; the loader must reject missing or stale required hold evidence. An empty optional registry that silently bypasses these obligations is unacceptable.

Apply report-level holds only to non-gold supervised loss after study labels and correspondence masking are assembled. Keep official gold values, mask1 and evaluation-only split intact; preserve exclusion reasons for audit without overriding gold. Preserve leakage graph edges from excluded reports. Validate holds consistently across exact normalized-report groups and explicitly assess the known near-report edge. No held target may be re-enabled by a later transformation, augmentation, data loader or loss function.

Required validation before policy acceptance: independent review of the hold semantics and finite evidence mapping; targeted tests for missing/stale/duplicate/unknown holds, changed correction scope, gold precedence, whole-report exclusions, duplicate propagation and target-order correctness; a complete non-gold coverage/class-count table by split and target, including changes caused by each hold category; final observed evidence validity and no material unexplained class collapse. Then complete the actual final-input optimizer/resume/inference acceptance already required. Lower coverage is a measured tradeoff to test during phase-two supervision ablations, not proof of superior labels or Kaggle performance.

No further blanket clinical reread, forced agreement or rewritten initial judgment is required merely to decide that an unresolved observation should not be used in the supervised loss. This design does not automatically discharge any existing gate; acceptance needs the concrete ledger and executed validation described above.
