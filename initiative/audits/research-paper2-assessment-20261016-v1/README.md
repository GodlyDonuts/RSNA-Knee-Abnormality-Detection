## Corrections that change decisions

1. **Adjacency is a hypothesis, not the diagnosed root cause.** Prior original-source review documents concatenated volume centers crossing slot boundaries and missing regions; the public94-window pipeline does not by itself establish94 physically source-adjacent triplets. Verify every public preprocessing/indexing stage before describing this challenger as exact reproduction. See docs/research-watch/additional/rsna-window-bag-mechanisms-2026-10-14.md and issue93. Wider source spacing can also supply useful context; the sign of a matched change is unknown.
2. **Population counts are stale.**356 metadata candidates include11 report-correspondence exclusions.345 additional eligible candidates imply2592 fitting,3887 train-report-eligible, potentially4327 frozen-final including440 eligible weakval. These are capacity/correspondence bounds, not automatic per-cell label admission. Source coverage is not accuracy. New345 paired256/384 cache job931411 is already running and must not be repeated.
3. **Cached rewindowing is approximate; physical recropping cannot restore detail.** The uint16 cache already underwent min/max scaling, spatial resampling and padding. Exclude original padding from percentile estimation using a justified valid support; distinguish a crop of an existing resized384 image from a140mm crop before resizing. Geometry/orientation and per-slice slope/intercept assumptions need actual checks. Do not describe these as lossless raw-preprocessing equivalence.
4. **Internal AUC is not public-equivalent AUC.** An internal0.915 threshold cannot predict a Kaggle0.92 score. A secondary dense-source endpoint measures agreement with report-derived supervisory targets, not independent clinical truth; the upstream source also used gold-guided choices. Declare its roster/masks/soft-vs-binary interpretation before inspecting outer source targets. Do not open gold for iterative sanity checks.
5. **Public recipes differ in several coupled ways.** Public0.949 uses different labels/cohort, including OAI, Gold-guided selection, crop, averaging and mirror inference. It is not clean causal evidence for any single step or a promise of+0.025–0.055 from adjacency. Fixed unvalidated AUC/rank-correlation gates are planning preferences, not statistical guarantees or reasons to stall useful comparisons.
6. **BN treatment is experimental.** Integer counters must not be averaged as floats. However, averaging floating running statistics is a legitimate explicit SWA policy, and is what the audited public author described. Retaining one checkpoint's buffers and recalibrating BN are different recipes, not automatically superior. The predeclared fixed14/15/16 candidate requires its own exact artifact and qualification.
7. **Mirror semantics need precision.** A correctly defined anatomical reflection does not automatically exchange medial/lateral disease labels. Plane, laterality, channel order and orientation determine whether the transform is valid. Generic width flips may hurt but are not proof that labels must swap.
8. **MRI-domain backbones require an actual input/checkpoint contract.** Do not assume OrthoFoundation accepts CoAtNet-style normalized3-slice RGB windows or plugs unchanged into our DINO adapter graph. Verify architecture, preprocessing, source overlap and checkpoint terms first.
9. **Deployment evidence has advanced.** Exact raw public MRI-to-CSV already passed2T4 in46.27686s; the distinct64-fitting-study/16chunk raw benchmark has now COMPLETED in318.49731105s, summed lifetimeRSS11,608,285,184B, zero unusable-study fallbacks. This is stronger runtime evidence, not a hidden-scale guarantee or clinical result. Existing diagnostic checkpoint never becomes a competition candidate.
10. **The serial calendar is too slow.** We already predeclared rawN3E4/8/12/16 Kaggle comparisons before CoAtNet accuracy results. Do not wait untilOct19 to deploy this family or until all unrelated research finishes. Preserve current training, implement adjacency independently, and prepare epoch components now.
