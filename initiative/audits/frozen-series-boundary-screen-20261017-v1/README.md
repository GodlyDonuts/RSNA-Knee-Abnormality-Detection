## Result and decision

All **3,887 fitting studies** had crossing windows: mean **8.0003 of 94**, max 9.
Removing these retained a full-parent fallback for an empty bag; no study needed
that fallback. Every case also admitted a deterministic count-matched interior
removal control. On **49,822 deterministic unique study pairs**:

| Quantity, range across 12 targets | Within-series removal | Same-count interior removal |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean removed attention mass | 0.35–1.54% | 1.57–9.96% |
| Mean absolute probability change | 0.00044–0.00211 | 0.00183–0.00756 |
| Conditional strict-pair ordering reversals | 0.074–0.494% | 0.321–1.511% |

The four cross-plane centers and two mixed-mirror-rule centers were separately
screened. Their all-target aggregates remain in the terminal. The original
parent already downweights boundary windows strongly. This **deprioritizes a
new boundary-only training arm**; it does not prove the existing policy improves
or harms disease AUC. Rare large changes remain: Baker's maximum absolute change
was **0.15370**, despite a small average. A deterministic interior removal is
count-matched, not an unbiased random-removal estimate or clinical control.

This is a readout counterfactual on existing features, not newly rendered
within-series triples. Both within-series and edge-repeat public notebooks
already passed public MRI acceptance earlier; neither has a competition score.
Do not recreate them or present this as a newly discovered source bug.
